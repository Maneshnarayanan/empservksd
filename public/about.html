About Employees Service, Kasaragod

Employees Service, Kasaragod is a dedicated resource portal designed to facilitate quick, organized, and reliable access to essential digital services for state government employees, teachers, aided school staff, and pensioners in Kasaragod district and across Kerala.

Our Mission & Purpose

Navigating across scattered state portals—ranging from payroll, taxation, and treasury systems to general education registries—can frequently be cumbersome. Our portal aggregates these essential resources into one centralized directory, categorizing services logically according to everyday administrative workflows.

Service Directory

Unified navigation to SPARK, BIMS, e-Treasury, Tax, Education, and KITE portals.

Resource Downloads

Access to official circulars, forms, orders, and salary templates.
