Policy

Privacy Policy & Terms of Service

Effective Date: October 2026

At employeeserviceksd.com, we are committed to upholding the privacy and data security of all visitors, government employees, and administrators accessing our community portal.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

This portal is designed exclusively as an open public resource directory and internal community blog. There are no public user accounts, no employee registration forms, and no personal data collection mechanisms for general visitors.

2. Cookies and Session Storage

We use strictly necessary HTTP session cookies solely for authenticated administrators to maintain secure administrative dashboard sessions and CSRF protection. Public visitors browsing service categories, downloading circulars, or reading blog articles do not require cookies.

3. External Links

Our service directory provides links to official external portals (such as SPARK, BIMS, e-Treasury, TRACES, etc.). When clicking an external link, you will be redirected to the respective governmental domain, which is governed by its own independent privacy and terms of service policies.
